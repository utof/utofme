# UI review — lens: breaker-mobile

Read-only pass over phone and tablet layouts. No product code was changed.

## Scope and method (read this first)

- **The live site could not be reached.** The egress proxy answered `403` to `CONNECT utofme.vip-ilyabondar.workers.dev:443` (policy denial, recorded in `/__agentproxy/status`). Per the proxy README, the denial was not retried or routed around.
- **What was reviewed instead:** a local build of `origin/main` (`packages/site`, `bun run build` with `UTOFME_FIXTURES=false`, matching `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`), served with `astro preview`. The `check:no-fixtures` guard passed on that build (23 pages). Owner: please confirm each item on the live URL before fixing.
- **Browser:** Playwright Chromium, headless, device emulation with touch and device-pixel-ratio 2–3. This is not iOS Safari or Android Chrome.
  - Viewports: iPhone 15 portrait (393×852) and landscape (852×393); Pixel 8 profile (412×915, Pixel 7 UA); iPad mini portrait (768×1024) and landscape (1024×768).
  - 36 URLs were probed on all five configs: home, all 7 deployed project pages, `/works/`, the 6 garden notes, `/garden/graph/`, `/now/`, `/uses/`, `/colophon/`, `/tops/`, `/stats/`, `/search/`, `/lab/datamosh/`.
  - Interaction checks: card tap → project page → Back; Pause motion (persistence across reload); reduced motion; orientation change; the banner crop across all 19 project banners.
- **Fonts did not load.** Astro's Google Fonts fetch failed during the sandbox build (`Could not fetch from https://fonts.google.com/metadata/fonts`). Screenshots therefore show fallback fonts. **No typography findings are made here.**
- **`/works/` → `/` redirect is not verified.** `public/_redirects` contains `/works /  301` and `/works/ /  301`, but `astro preview` ignores `_redirects` and returns 404 locally. Owner: confirm the 301 on the live site.
- **Not tested:** touch-drag on the Datamosh lab, tapping graph nodes, and iOS input-zoom (inferred from the 16px rule below).
- **Evidence folder:** `packages/specs/reviews/ui/breaker-mobile/`. Screenshots are at DPR 2 and scaled to CSS pixels (≤1280 px wide).

## Checked and fine

- **No horizontal page overflow** on any probed page, in portrait or landscape, on iPhone 15, the Pixel 8 profile, or iPad mini.
- **Pinch zoom** is allowed: the viewport meta is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1` on every page, with no `user-scalable=no`.
- **Phone banner aspect** is 16:9 (329×185 at iPhone 15, 348×196 at Pixel 8) as specified. Tablet portrait and landscape are 3:1.
- **Banner resolution** is 1920×1080 on all banner encodes (the owner's re-encode).
- **Card tap → project page → Back:** the card tap navigates to `/works/…`, and Back returns to `/` at the same scroll position (660 px before and after).
- **Pause motion:** every loop pauses, the label flips to "Play motion", and the state survives a reload. The button is reachable on the home page.
- **Reduced motion** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`): loops stay paused at t=0 and stills are shown. Card zoom is `transform: none`.
- **Orientation change** (portrait → landscape on the same page): re-lays out without overflow; the banner resizes.
- **Gallery** on phone: single column of 329 px frames at 16:9, captions 12 px, and prev/next present.
- **Footer identity text** is inset 12–29 px on every page; I did not reproduce the left-edge footer block. The left-edge item on the garden graph is a graph node, not the footer (see B-17).
- **Console:** no page errors on any probed page. Failed resources are the placeholder avatar on `/garden/welcome/` (B-04). The 404s on `/works/` and on fixture slugs (`/works/code-1/` etc.) are expected: the deploy build excludes fixtures, and the `/works/` redirect is unverified locally (see scope notes).

## Top 10 to fix first

1. **B-01** — Stray cursor ring at the top-left of every page on touch devices. Hide the cursor island on coarse pointers.
2. **B-02** — Garden pages (index, notes, graph) have no side gutter and sit flush to the left edge.
3. **B-04** — `/garden/welcome/` shows test webmentions ("Alex Example", "Sam Reply") with a broken avatar. Remove or gate the fixture file.
4. **B-06** — Tap targets: Pause motion (105×28), sound toggle (32×32), footer "source" (39×20), "All projects" and prev/next (20 px tall).
5. **B-05** — On landscape phones the project banner takes 60% of the screen and pushes the title below the fold. Cap its height on short viewports.
6. **B-03** — Side gutters differ by page (0, 12, 29, 32 px). Use one page-margin wrapper everywhere.
7. **B-07 / B-09** — Datamosh lab: 13–14px controls (iOS zoom on focus) and a sticky diagnostics panel that is taller than the viewport on short screens.
8. **B-10** — Caption meta line and "private for now" at 11px, below the 12px floor.
9. **B-12** — Phone banner crops cut chart labels and figure edges (`no-link`, `modified-maxwell-taichi`, `math-2`). Add a horizontal focus per work.
10. **B-14** — During the card → banner transition, the root cross-fade shows the masonry and the project text overlapping for part of the 450 ms morph.

Also worth doing with these: B-11 (search has no header) and B-13 (10 fps banner encodes, if not intended).
