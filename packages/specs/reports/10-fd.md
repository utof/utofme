## Decisions and conflicts
- F4 stamp lives inside the figure (keyframe and island) because it changes with the block selector; the Block does not pass `measuredOn`.
- Fact `tempo.contact.before12.agents` = 80 % (2026-11-02 data) but the 2026-11-04 rerun distribution gives 79.5 %, so the figure sentence prints "79 %". Prose (MDX) says 80 %; owner/lane P to reconcile (re-measure the fact, or accept).
- F11 5v5 rows carry no intervals for either side (per spec); pros' intervals exist in the file for some but are not shown, to keep the row family uniform.
- F11 grenade/flash agent intervals are the means of the summed per-side bounds (documented in the dataset module).
- Agent series colour reuses `--color-ct` (no agent token exists); pros are black diamonds, bots grey squares.
- No `motionState()` in the three islands: nothing animates (instant state changes), so Pause motion/reduced motion hold trivially.
- F8 width is `text` per spec (the stub had `wide`). F8 stamp is the `peek.hit.nosnap` measuredOn ("block 28 (5v5), 2026-11-02").
- F8 model: 8-tile corridor, holder tile 8 aimed at tiles 4-5, peeker from tile 3 in 0.8 s steps; delayed shot lands on the vacated tile; snap shot on arrival into the aim cone. Real CS: `cs2.tickrate` = 64 gives 15.6 ms and 51 checks per tick.
- Caption/try lines in the MDX match what the figures do; F4's caption only gives the agents' median, consistent with guess-first.
- Island fixed heights: F8 root is a fixed 31rem (keyframe strip and island), F4 footer min 19rem/15rem, F11 rows border-box; keyframe and island heights are equal on phone and desktop.
