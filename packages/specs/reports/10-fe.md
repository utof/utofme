## Decisions and conflicts
- AC10.1 "Next x9 reaches 9 / 9": the default is stage 4 and Next stops at 9 (button disabled), so five presses reach 9 / 9; the e2e presses Next only while enabled, then Previous back to 1 / 9.
- F6 width is `full` (spec), below 1200 px the article layout already stops constraining full-width figures; the reserved box is content-sized (diagram + fixed-height footer) identical in keyframe and island instead of a fixed aspect ratio, because the vertical layout is taller than the wide one.
- F6 vertical layout (second coordinates, 360 x 792, two columns) lives in `machine.ts`. Long arrows still run behind boxes; the diagram is as legible as the data's own layout allows.
- Edges: tab order is all 18 boxes in reading order, then all 27 arrows (arrows are drawn behind boxes, so their focusable layer comes last). From an arrow, Left/Up focuses its source box and Right/Down its target.
- F7 geometry is computed from all entries, so filtering removes marks without moving the rest. Marks of one area and day are spread across the day and stacked into lanes.
- Opening a card from the list for an entry the active chips hide clears the chips.
- F7 phone layout has its own fixed-height scroll region (36 rem) so hydration does not shift the page; chips use a taller fixed area at <= 1099 / 700 px so all 12 area chips stay reachable.
- F7 "Parked" drawer label shows the count before it is opened (prop `parkedCount`, 74).
- AC11.7: unit test resolves every `markers[].notebookId` of `elo.json` (p3-flash-arc, p3-bot-roles, p3-kick-fixed) to an entry, and `#nb-<id>` opens that card (unit and e2e).
- Caption and try lines in the MDX match both figures; the MDX sentence "Ten entries carry most of the story, and they are starred" matches the ring marker, which is also in the legend.
