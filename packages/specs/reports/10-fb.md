## Design decisions the spec left open

- Facing of a token is part of the literal scene and is not changed by dragging (a drag only moves the position). `R` / Shift+`R` turns a focused token and Enter says whom it sees; both are extras, listed in the figure's description.
- The ghost is a button at the end of the Tab order and a click target on the whole disc (mouse and touch); "Add smoke" drops the next smoke on the ghost first, then on two other spots; double-click or Delete removes a smoke.
- Heights are reserved by construction (fixed or minimum row heights, `calc(100cqw + …)` for the stacked layouts) so the SSR keyframe and the island occupy the same box at 320 to 1440 px; measured equal at nine widths for each figure.
- F5's morph is a per-square opacity cross-fade (transform/opacity only), instant under Pause motion or reduced motion; stage 3 rings the squares the corridor pass reopened and stage 4 crosses the squares it closed (derived from the rows; the closed count is not a dataset field).
- F10 fetches both datasets on mount (the Mirage file is the one F2 loads, so on the real page it is one request); a missing Mirage only disables Compare.

## Doubts and issues

2. RollMap's exclusive JS on the lab page is 5.7 of 6.0 KB (R10 warning). It is enforced for `labFigure` routes, so a few hundred bytes of growth would fail the lab build; the article page has plenty of room.
3. Article HTML is 35.7 KB with F1 and my three figures; the 15 % warning starts at 38.25 KB and the limit is 45 KB. Lanes FC to FE should check their keyframes (merged rectangles and `<symbol>` helped here).
4. Copy (lane P's file, not edited): the page says "a player standing inside a smoke can still be seen from outside it". The rule is the "legs" rule: a smoke on a sample tile blocks unless that tile is the target's, and a smoke centred on the target covers the tiles next to it, so the target is visible only from the rim side of the disc. Suggest "...can still be seen from the smoke's edge", or keep and rely on the figure's description. The caption and Try lines of F2, F5 and F10 otherwise match what the figures do.
5. F5 stage 2 is the project's floor-fraction grid, a 28 x 28 digit grid transformed from the overhead picture, shipped on purpose (OD-5, spec risk 10); the source file's own licence note says to check before publishing floor masks, so the owner may want to confirm. Stage 1 exists in no shipped file.
6. The e2e timing check (100 synthetic moves) uses the best of three batches (median at most 1 ms, 95th percentile at most 4 ms) because the shared runner is noisy; quiet-machine median was 0.5 ms.
7. The two dataset property tests used 28 x 28 grids built per sample and timed out once under load; they now draw from pools of pre-sampled grids (fixed seed).
