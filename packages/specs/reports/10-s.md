## Doubts and conflicts
- Spec silence: the `maps_cs` wall and floor glyphs are not specified. I assumed `#` wall and `.` floor, with `a/b/t/c` as in the spec. `parseRows` throws on any other glyph. If the real files differ (for example `X`), change `GLYPH` in `grid.ts`; D2/Lane I will find out immediately.
- `parseRows` expands an `A`/`B` centre to its 3×3 only onto plain floor tiles; walls and spawns inside the 3×3 stay. The spec says "site = the 3×3 around it" and does not cover this.
- `smokes` is a `ReadonlySet<number>` of flat tile indices (spec shows only the name); `smokeTiles` returns flat indices. `visiblePairs` takes `{r, c, facing, alive}`.
- `rovingTabindex` returns the next index, wrapping on arrows. The spec gives only the signature.
- `figure-scale.ts` is not in this lane's list and is not built here.
- Extra exports beyond the spec's fixed names: `CELL`, `SiteRect`, `Tile`, `Viewer`, `Player`, `inVisionCone`.
