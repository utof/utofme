A desktop Godot playground for wormholes, light paths and causal loops {/* src: README.md:3 */}. Eight labs run from Basics to Rooms, and in each one an equation card drives the scene: drag a cyan symbol to change the world, and amber results update as you go {/* src: README.md:14-15; README.md:41-44 */}.

The Sky and Rooms views trace light per pixel on the GPU {/* src: README.md:23 */}. The Black holes lab runs from slow clocks and GPS to the inside of a spinning hole {/* src: README.md:139-158 */}. A badge in each lab says what is and isn't simulated {/* src: README.md:53-54 */}.
