Animated derivations from Cartan geometry, made in 2022 {/* src: media/videos (file mtimes 2022-07-30 to 2022-10-27) */}. Each scene is a `%%manim` cell in a Jupyter notebook: the tetrad postulate, a covariant derivative expanded with the spin connection, the Jacobian from Cartesian to polar coordinates, a tangent plane on a sphere. Equations rewrite in place with `TransformMatchingTex`, so every step shows which symbols moved {/* src: test.ipynb cell cov_der2, lines 11, 31 */}.

The main notebook holds 29 scenes, and the render folder holds 49 clips at 1080p60 from Manim Community v0.16 {/* src: test.ipynb (29 %%manim cells, counted); media/videos/manimce/1080p60 (49 .mp4, counted); media/images/manimce/evans_ManimCE_v0.16.0.post0.png */}. It has no repository.
