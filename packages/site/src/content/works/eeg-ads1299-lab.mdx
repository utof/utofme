A Python lab for learning an EEG front end before buying anything {/* src: README.md:3 */}. It generates EEG-like signals, runs them through an illustrative electrode circuit and signed 24-bit conversion, checks signal quality and ends with a small machine-learning experiment {/* src: README.md:3 */}. A Rev A baseline picks an ADS1299-4 board with an ESP32-S3, and the firmware is a bench-only starter {/* src: README.md:5 */}.

It is plain about what it is not: not a finished EEG recorder, not a validated PCB, and not permission to connect a circuit to a person {/* src: README.md:5 */}.
