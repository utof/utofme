A top-down grid shooter in pure JAX, modelled on Counter-Strike: 28×28 tiles, bomb defuse, fog of war, smokes and molotovs. {/* src: STATUS.md:21-23 */} Agents learn by PPO self-play on 2,048 generated maps and are scored on 5 maps cut from CS2 radars, with Mirage held out. {/* src: JOURNAL.md:15; STATUS.md:26; STATUS.md:43-44 */}

After 28 training blocks the 5v5 agent sits +96 Elo (+94..+98) above its starting point. {/* src: JOURNAL.md:6082; JOURNAL.md:6090; JOURNAL.md:5413 */} It got stronger, not more CS-like: first contact still comes at 9.6 s, against 22.8 s for CS2 pros. {/* src: CS2_ROUNDS_REPORT.md:15; JOURNAL.md:6396 */} Closing that gap needs game changes, not more training. {/* src: JOURNAL.md:6399 */}
