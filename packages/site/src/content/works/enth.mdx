A modular synthesizer in Rust, released under MIT and shipped first as a CLAP plugin, with a game built in. {/* src: docs/README.md:7; LICENSE:1 */} Recreate generates a random patch at the difficulty you pick; you rebuild it by ear and get a score. {/* src: docs/README.md:9 */} Learn teaches sound design on the same instrument. Sound → patch turns a sample into editable patches with a CMA-ES search. {/* src: docs/README.md:11-12; docs/README.md:31 */}

It is also built for AI agents: through a CLI, an LLM can create a patch, render it, read how it sounds and adjust it toward a goal. {/* src: docs/README.md:14 */}
