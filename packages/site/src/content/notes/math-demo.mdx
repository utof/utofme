Einstein's mass-energy equivalence: $E = mc^2$.

The integral of a parabola over the unit interval:

$$\int_0^1 x^2\,dx = \tfrac{1}{3}$$

Math blocks are only rendered with KaTeX on notes that declare `math: true` in
frontmatter — this keeps the KaTeX CSS (~24 KB) off math-free notes.
